# SkinDeep

## 1.1.0

First public release.

- 19 damage skins for the floating combat numbers:
  - **Class skins**: Bloodrage (Warrior), Lightbringer (Paladin), Dead Eye (Hunter),
    Cutthroat (Rogue), Halo (Priest), Stormcall (Shaman), Spellweave (Mage),
    Fel Pact (Warlock) and Wild Bloom (Druid).
  - **Auto: my class** (the default) picks your character's class skin.
  - **Jackpot**: slot-machine rolls on every hit; crits on your target open a
    jackpot cabinet that lands on your crit and pays out in coins.
  - Classic+, Ember Tide, Frostbite, Arcane Sigil, Kaboom!, Bloodmoon Ink,
    Retro Arcade, Gilded Hoard and Swiftblade.
- Physical and spell damage in distinct colors, with three schemes: skin
  palette, Classic (white/yellow) and spell schools.
- Replaces Blizzard's floating damage numbers, or restyles them with the skin's
  font and motion.
- Stays on in groups by default, with an option to hand back to Blizzard's
  numbers while grouped.
- Settings window with a live preview (`/skindeep`).
