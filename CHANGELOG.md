# SkinDeep

## 1.3.0

- **PvP ranks now match the Legacy ladder** (Rank 3, 7, 10, 13, 14):
  - New **Sergeant** skins for each faction (Rank 3), and **Field Marshal**
    and **Warlord** (Rank 13), each with its own crit title.
  - Knight and Stone Guard become **Knight-Lieutenant** and **Blood Guard**
    at Rank 7. Already earned? They stay yours.
- **Fifteen secret skins** on a new **Fun** tab, each earned by a secret
  feat. The Feats tab's **Secrets** group shows only a riddle until you earn
  one:
  - Natural 20, Synthwave, Glitch, Disco Fever, Bunny Hop, Graveyard Shift
    and Hogger!
  - Anime-inspired: Power Surge, Final Move, Sakura Drift and Mecha Strike.
  - Music-inspired: Bass Drop, Lo-Fi, Karaoke and Power Ballad.
- The Rank 3 to 14 PvP skins show the Alliance and Horde crests.

## 1.2.1

- **Skin sharing fix**: hovering players no longer fills chat with "No
  player named ... is currently playing". SkinDeep now asks players by their
  full name (first name and surname), only asks your own faction, asks the
  same player at most every few minutes, and hides the game's not-found
  line for its own requests.
- `/skindeep peek` takes a full name (Haze-Grimm) when two SkinDeep users
  share a first name.
- Echo hits (Rampage, Stormstrike) land a little further from the first hit,
  so they no longer overlap on crits.

## 1.2.0

Collect your skins.

- **First-run choice**, like Forever Dungeon Journal: play as a **Collector**
  (start with Classic+, Auto and your class's skin, earn the rest) or have
  **Everything unlocked**. Change it any time on the new Feats tab.
- **Feats**: SkinDeep's own achievements unlock skins. Land 777 crits for
  Jackpot, deal Fire damage for Ember Tide, earn gold for Gilded Hoard, reach
  level 10 on a class for its skin, and more.
- **Six faction skins**, each earned by a faction feat:
  - **Crimson Oath** (Scarlet Crusade): numbers march up over the crusade's
    sigil; crits burn with holy fire.
  - **Plague Tide** (Scourge): numbers lurch through plague mist; crits raise
    an icy rune and a skull.
  - **Red Kerchief** (Defias Brotherhood): numbers pop up from below; crits go
    off in a gunpowder flash and smoke.
  - **Molten Anvil** (Dark Iron): molten numbers slam down and throw forge
    sparks.
  - **Broodmother** (Black Dragonflight): violet numbers rise; crits breathe a
    cone of dragonfire.
  - **Silver Dawn** (Argent Dawn): silver numbers rise over the dawn light;
    crits break into rays.
- **Ten PvP skins**:
  - **Rank ladders** that follow your PvP rank (Rank 6, 10 and 14), for each faction: Knight,
    Lieutenant Commander and **Grand Marshal** (Alliance); Stone Guard,
    Champion and **High Warlord** (Horde). The Rank 14 skins are the most
    extravagant in SkinDeep: an aura on every hit, and crits with a title
    banner, layered bursts, confetti or fire and skulls, and (High Warlord)
    a triple war-drum pulse.
  - **Flag Runner** (win 5 Warsong Gulch), **Five Banners** (win 5 Arathi
    Basin), **Winter Siege** (win 3 Alterac Valley), **Duelist** (win 25 duels).
- **Sixteen more skins**:
  - **Styles**: Starfall (100 crits at night), Gearworks (100 Mechanical
    foes), Murloc Mayhem (100 Murlocs), and three seasonal skins earned by
    playing in their month: Hallow's End (October), Winter Veil (December)
    and Heartthrob (February).
  - **Factions**: Broadside (Bloodsail Buccaneers), Hexbound (Gurubashi),
    Old God Whispers (Twilight's Hammer), Silithid Swarm (Qiraji) and
    Tidecaller (Hydraxian Waterlords).
  - **Honorable kills**: Tally Marks (100), Lion's Pride (250 as Alliance),
    Blood & Thunder (250 as Horde), Headhunter (1,000) and Warmonger (5,000).
    Kills made before installing SkinDeep count, from each character's
    lifetime total.
- **28 spec skins**, one for each talent tree of every class (plus Guardian
  for Druids): each is a twist on its class skin. Earn one by spending
  **20 talent points** in that tree on a character of the class; a respec
  never takes it back. **Auto: my class** uses the skin of the tree you have
  the most points in, once you have earned it. Feral Combat earns both Shred
  and Ironbark, and Auto uses Ironbark in Bear Form. On the Skins tab, click a
  class to fold out its specs. `/skindeep talents` shows the points SkinDeep
  reads.
- **12 profession skins** that grow with your skill: Philter (Alchemy),
  Anvilstrike (Blacksmithing), Runethread (Enchanting), Blastcap
  (Engineering), Wildroot (Herbalism), Stitchwork (Leatherworking),
  Motherlode (Mining), Keen Edge (Skinning), Bolt of Cloth (Tailoring),
  Sizzle (Cooking), Lucky Bobber (Fishing) and Field Dressing (First Aid).
  Reaching **150** skill unlocks the skin (Journeyman), **225** upgrades it
  to Expert (its signature crit) and **300** to Artisan (effects on every
  hit, bigger crits and an Artisan title). Skill counts from any character.
  They have their own **Trades** tab; on the Feats tab, step through the
  tiers.
- **Five loot skins** on a new **Loot** tab: Fresh Find (loot an Uncommon
  item), Rare Find (Rare or better), Epic Haul (Epic or better: a beam of
  light on crits), Legendary (a pillar of light, rays and a LEGENDARY banner)
  and Full Set (wear every piece of an item set: crits snap the set pieces
  together and call SET BONUS). `/skindeep loot` shows what SkinDeep reads.
- **Polish pass on every skin**:
  - Crits land with a springy punch and flash bright as they hit; numbers
    hold full strength longer, then shrink a touch as they fade.
  - Sparks and debris slow as they fly and shrink as they fade.
  - Effects behind a number stay steady and fade out with it.
  - Echo hits (Rampage, Stormstrike) land clearly beside the first.
  - Easier-to-read numbers on Arcane Sigil, Arcane Surge, Fel Pact,
    Gearworks, Hallow's End, Stormcall and Thunderhead; a stronger crack
    for Bloodrage; Rank 14 skins that frame the number instead of burying
    it; and fuller crits for Silithid Swarm and Fresh Find.
- **Skin sharing**: SkinDeep users see each other's skins. Hover a player
  to see theirs on the tooltip ("SkinDeep: Grand Marshal"), and
  `/skindeep peek` (your target, or a name) plays it in the preview, even
  a skin you haven't unlocked. In a **duel** with another SkinDeep user, the
  hits you take show in your opponent's skin, and yours show in your skin on
  their screen. Turn it off with Share skins with players.
- **PvP skin**: Shift-click a skin to use it automatically in battlegrounds and
  against players (`/skindeep pvp <name|off>`).
- **Unlock popups** with the achievement fanfare (both can be turned off).
- The window has **Skins** and **Feats** tabs, each grouped into Styles,
  Classes, Factions and PvP. Locked skins show what earns them with your
  progress, and can still be previewed.
- Progress and unlocks are shared by all your characters.
- New commands: `/skindeep feats`, `/skindeep welcome`, `/skindeep pvp`,
  `/skindeep rank`, `/skindeep talents`, `/skindeep loot`, `/skindeep peek`.

## 1.1.0

First public release.

- 19 damage skins for the floating combat numbers:
  - **Class skins**: Bloodrage (Warrior), Lightbringer (Paladin), Dead Eye (Hunter),
    Cutthroat (Rogue), Halo (Priest), Stormcall (Shaman), Spellweave (Mage),
    Fel Pact (Warlock) and Wild Bloom (Druid).
  - **Auto: my class** (the default) picks your character's class skin.
  - **Jackpot**: slot-machine rolls on every hit; crits on your target open a
    jackpot cabinet that lands on your crit and pays out in coins.
  - Classic+, Ember Tide, Frostbite, Arcane Sigil, Kaboom!, Bloodmoon Ink,
    Retro Arcade, Gilded Hoard and Swiftblade.
- Physical and spell damage in distinct colors, with three schemes: skin
  palette, Classic (white/yellow) and spell schools.
- Replaces Blizzard's floating damage numbers, or restyles them with the skin's
  font and motion.
- Stays on in groups by default, with an option to hand back to Blizzard's
  numbers while grouped.
- Settings window with a live preview (`/skindeep`).
