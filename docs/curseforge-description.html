SkinDeep

Damage skins for the floating combat numbers in WoW: Forever.

SkinDeep turns the numbers that pop over your enemies into animated, themed damage skins: custom fonts, motion, colors, crit bursts and particles. Every hit has a little style, and every crit lands with a punch.

Type /skindeep to open the skin picker with a live preview. 110+ skins across Styles, Classes, Factions, PvP, Trades, Loot and a tab of secrets, and no two alike: wings unfold, vines burst into flower, storm clouds break and arrows thud in beside your numbers.

Collect them, or have them all

The first time you log in, choose how to play:

  • Collector: start with Classic+, Auto and your class's skin, and earn the rest through SkinDeep's own feats.
  • Everything unlocked: every skin right away (feats still pop up for fun).

Switch any time on the Feats tab, which shows your progress. Unlocks are shared by all your characters, and each unlock pops up with the achievement fanfare.

Every class, every spec

SkinDeep starts on Auto: my class, so each character gets its own look: Bloodrage (Warrior), Lightbringer (Paladin), Dead Eye (Hunter), Cutthroat (Rogue), Halo (Priest), Stormcall (Shaman), Spellweave (Mage), Fel Pact (Warlock) and Wild Bloom (Druid).

28 spec skins put a twist on each class skin, one per talent tree (plus Guardian for Druids): Venom drips poison, Seraph spreads its wings, Thunderhead calls down lightning and rain, Bullseye lands an arrow, Lifebloom grows a flowering vine, plus Mortal Strike, Shield Wall, Pyroclasm, Glacial Spike, Starsurge and more. Spend 20 talent points in a tree to earn its skin, and Auto switches to the tree you have the most points in.

Factions

Crimson Oath (Scarlet Crusade), Plague Tide (Scourge), Red Kerchief (Defias), Molten Anvil (Dark Iron), Broodmother (Black Dragonflight), Silver Dawn (Argent Dawn), Broadside (Bloodsail), Hexbound (Gurubashi), Old God Whispers (Twilight's Hammer), Silithid Swarm (Qiraji) and Tidecaller (Hydraxian Waterlords). Each is earned by slaying that faction's foes or defeating its boss.

PvP

  • Rank ladders that follow the Legacy PvP ranks (3, 7, 10, 13 and 14), each flying the Alliance or Horde crest: Sergeant, Knight-Lieutenant, Lieutenant Commander, Field Marshal and Grand Marshal; Sergeant, Blood Guard, Champion, Warlord and High Warlord. Every rank crits its own way: the crest stamps down, a banner unrolls, swords cross, claws carve the crest, war paint swipes, spears lift the crest, a wall of fire rises. The Rank 14 skins are the most extravagant in SkinDeep.
  • Battlegrounds and duels: Flag Runner, Five Banners, Winter Siege and Duelist.
  • Honorable kills: Tally Marks, Lion's Pride (a roar rolls out from the Alliance crest), Blood & Thunder (Horde war drums and lightning), Headhunter and Warmonger. Kills from before you installed SkinDeep count.
  • PvP skin: shift-click any skin to use it in battlegrounds and against players.

Trades

A skin for every profession that grows with your skill: unlock it at 150 (Journeyman), upgrade it at 225 (Expert) and 300 (Artisan, with an Artisan title on crits). Philter, Anvilstrike, Runethread, Blastcap, Wildroot, Stitchwork, Motherlode, Keen Edge, Bolt of Cloth, Sizzle, Lucky Bobber and Field Dressing.

Loot

Fresh Find, Rare Find (its crits turn a sapphire), Epic Haul and Legendary unlock when you loot an item of that quality. Full Set unlocks when you wear every piece of an item set; its crits call SET BONUS.

Secrets

The Fun tab holds 15 secret skins: tabletop dice, 80s neon, VHS glitches, disco, anime power-ups and cut-ins, lo-fi beats, karaoke, power ballads and one very angry gnoll. Each is earned by a secret feat, and the Feats tab gives you only a riddle. "Let the dice decide." "Every floor is a dance floor." "Elwynn's worst nightmare." The rest you'll have to find.

Jackpot! and the styles

Every Jackpot hit is a little slot roll that lands on your real damage; crit your target and a full slot cabinet opens and pays out in gold coins (with sound). Plus Classic+, Ember Tide, Frostbite, Arcane Sigil, Kaboom!, Bloodmoon Ink, Retro Arcade, Gilded Hoard, Swiftblade, Starfall, Gearworks, Murloc Mayhem and three seasonal skins: Hallow's End, Winter Veil and Heartthrob.

See each other's skins

SkinDeep users see each other's skin on their tooltip, and /skindeep peek plays your target's skin in the preview. In a duel between two SkinDeep users, the hits you take show in your opponent's skin.

Options

  • Physical and spell damage always look different: each skin's own palette, Classic (white and yellow) or spell-school colors
  • Replace Blizzard's numbers, or restyle them with your skin's font and motion
  • Numbers on your target and other enemy nameplates (AoE), plus optional damage taken and heals received
  • Size, speed, bursts and particles, sounds, abbreviated big numbers, movable anchors

Commands: /skindeep, /skindeep feats, /skindeep test, /skindeep skin <name>, /skindeep pvp, /skindeep peek, /skindeep list, /skindeep off, /skindeep restore

Good to know

  • Groups: the game doesn't tell addons whose hit landed on your target, so in a party SkinDeep also shows your party's hits on your target. Prefer only your own? Tick Blizzard's numbers in groups.
  • Blizzard's font changes after your next login.
  • Clean uninstall: SkinDeep hands back every game setting it changes each time you log out, and /skindeep restore does it right away.

SkinDeep's fonts and art are original; the PvP skins show the game's own Alliance and Horde crests. Open source (MIT) on GitHub: github.com/Starden/SkinDeep
