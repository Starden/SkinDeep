SkinDeep

Damage skins for the floating combat numbers in WoW: Forever.

SkinDeep turns the numbers that pop over your enemies into animated, themed damage skins: custom fonts, motion, colors, crit bursts and particles. Every hit has a little style, and every crit is a moment.

Type /skindeep to open the skin picker with a live preview.

Every class gets its own skin

SkinDeep starts on Auto: my class, so each character gets its own look without touching a setting.

  • Bloodrage (Warrior): numbers slam down from above; crits crack the ground and throw debris.
  • Lightbringer (Paladin): radiant numbers rise in state; crits call down a column of light.
  • Dead Eye (Hunter): numbers fly in like arrows; crits snap a crosshair onto the target.
  • Cutthroat (Rogue): slanted numbers blink in and vanish; crits slash an X and spill poison.
  • Halo (Priest): soft glowing numbers float under a halo; crits shed feathers.
  • Stormcall (Shaman): crystal numbers crackle upward; crits fork chain lightning.
  • Spellweave (Mage): sparks converge and the number bursts out; crits ripple fire, frost and arcane.
  • Fel Pact (Warlock): flickering numbers over a fel sigil; crits flare fel fire.
  • Wild Bloom (Druid): numbers sway up like falling leaves; crits bloom a flower.

Jackpot!

Every hit is a little slot roll: random numbers blur past and land on your real damage. Crit your target and a full slot cabinet opens: the reel spins, clunks into place on your crit, and JACKPOT! pays out in a shower of gold coins (with sound).

And nine more styles

Classic+, Ember Tide, Frostbite, Arcane Sigil, Kaboom!, Bloodmoon Ink, Retro Arcade, Gilded Hoard and Swiftblade.

Physical vs. spell, at a glance

Physical and spell damage always look different. Pick a color scheme:

  • Skin palette: each skin's own matching pair
  • Classic: physical white, spell yellow, like Blizzard's
  • Spell schools: Fire orange, Frost blue, Shadow purple, and so on

Options

  • Replace Blizzard's numbers, show both, or just restyle Blizzard's own numbers with your skin's font and motion
  • Numbers on your target and on other enemy nameplates (AoE)
  • Optional numbers for damage you take and heals you receive
  • Size, speed, bursts and particles, sounds, abbreviated big numbers
  • Movable anchors for when a target has no nameplate

Commands: /skindeep, /skindeep test, /skindeep skin <name>, /skindeep list, /skindeep off, /skindeep restore

Good to know

  • Groups: the game doesn't tell addons whose hit landed on your target, so in a party SkinDeep also shows your party's hits on your target. Prefer only your own? Tick Blizzard's numbers in groups.
  • Blizzard's font changes after your next login.
  • Clean uninstall: SkinDeep hands back every game setting it changes each time you log out, and /skindeep restore does it right away.
